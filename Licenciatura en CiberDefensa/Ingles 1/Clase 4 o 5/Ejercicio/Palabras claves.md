## Phishing and Malware
In the **digital world**, there are many **dangers** we need to be careful of. Two common threats are phishing and malware.

Phishing is when someone tries to trick you into giving **personal information**, like your password or **credit card number**. They often send fake **emails** or **messages** that look real. These **messages** might say there is a **problem** with your bank account or that you won a prize. The **attacker** can steal your **information** if you **click** the **link** and **enter** your **details**.

**Malware** is short for “**malicious software**.” It is a type of bad **program** that can damage your **computer** or steal your **data**. You can get **malware** by downloading unknown files, clicking unsafe links, or **visiting** dangerous **websites**. Some common types of **malware are viruses, spyware, and ransomware**. **For example**, a virus can slow down your **computer**, and **ransomware** can lock your **files** until you pay **money**.

To stay safe, do not **click** on **links** or **download files** from people you don’t know. Always check if a **message** is real. Look for strange spellings or suspicious **email addresses**. Use **antivirus software** and **update** your devices often. Being careful **online helps protect your information**.

- Phishing - Similar a "pescar" en español, lo que ayuda a entender la idea de "engañar" o "atrapar" información.

- Malware - Compuesto por "mal" (malicioso) y "ware" (software), fácil de relacionar con software dañino.

- Digital - Muy similar al español "digital".

- Personal - Igual que en español ("personal").

- Information - Similar a "información".

- Password - Cercano a "contraseña" (aunque en español es más común "clave").

- Bank - Similar a "banco" o "banco" en español.

- Virus - Igual que en español ("virus").

- Computer - Similar a "computadora" (en Argentina se usa más "computadora" que "ordenador").

- Data - Similar a "datos".

- Link - Usado tal cual en español coloquial ("link" o "enlace").

- Email - Similar a "correo electrónico", pero ampliamente reconocido como "email".

- Software - Usado directamente en español.

- Antivirus - Igual que en español ("antivirus").

- Update - Similar a "actualizar" ("updatear" en jerga informática).
