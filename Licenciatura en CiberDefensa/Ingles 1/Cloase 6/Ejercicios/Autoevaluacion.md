# Preguntas Clase 6 - Phishing

### 1. Why do phishing attacks often increase around holidays or major shopping events like Prime Day?
**Respuesta:**  
Because people are more distracted and more likely to fall for scams related to shopping or special offers, making them easier targets for fraud.

---

### 2. What is phishing, and how does it differ from other types of cyberattacks?
**Respuesta:**  
Phishing is a type of cyberattack that tricks people into sharing sensitive information or downloading malware. Unlike other attacks that directly target systems, phishing relies on manipulating human error and deception.

---

### 3. Why is phishing considered a major cyberthreat despite advancements in cybersecurity tools?
**Respuesta:**  
Because it exploits people instead of technical flaws. Attackers don’t need to hack systems; they only need to deceive users with authorized access into doing harmful actions.

---

### 4. What role did phishing play in the 2016 U.S. presidential campaign involving Hillary Clinton?
**Respuesta:**  
Russian hackers used a fake password-reset email to steal thousands of emails from Clinton’s campaign, showing how effective phishing can be—even against trained staff.

---

### 5. How do scammers make phishing emails appear authentic to their victims?
**Respuesta:**  
They use real logos and branding, spoof email addresses, and copy genuine messages to make them look legitimate. They also use subject lines that trigger urgency or emotions.
